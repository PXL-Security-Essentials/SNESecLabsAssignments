# Test Endpoint Security
In this lab, you will create an antivirus test file to verify whether your endpoint protection solution detects it as malware. You will also check how many antivirus engines on VirusTotal identify the file as malicious.

## Connect to the Lab Environment
- Open the lab environment at http://x.x.x.x:8080
- Once the website loads, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the exercises.

> Your objective: Find all three flags hidden throughout the lab.
